# NutriFit Contributor Copyright Assignment Agreement 1.0

Version 1.0. Standing offer effective upon authorized publication by NUTRIFIT LLC through the official repository with its electronic acceptance process enabled. The accepted record includes this agreement and the exact grant-back license text. A commit signature, `Signed-off-by`, or acceptance of the repository license alone does not substitute for intentional electronic signing of this agreement. Mandatory additional formalities remain applicable.

## 1. Identified parties and contribution

Company: **NUTRIFIT LLC**, a Wyoming limited liability company, United States of America.

Contributor: the person identified by the authenticated GitHub account and acceptance evidence. The account identifier does not by itself prove legal ownership. An organization as rights owner, representative authority, or jurisdiction-specific identity requirements must be documented separately before merge when applicable. Additional personal information is collected privately only when necessary.

Project: `https://github.com/nutrifit-health/craving-engine`.

The "Contribution" is **only** material intentionally submitted by the agreeing Contributor for inclusion in this specified repository, with full commit SHA, pull request URL and file or patch scope identified in Schedule A. It includes new copyrightable code, documentation and associated assets that Contributor owns. Private local commits, unrelated repositories, unsubmitted private forks, research ideas, discussion comments and material submitted by other people are excluded.

Schedule A may be created automatically from an authenticated submission after an initial express electronic acceptance of the final agreement. An initial acceptance may authorize this defined process for subsequent intentional submissions under the same agreement version, to the extent applicable law permits. A materially changed agreement requires fresh acceptance. Jurisdictions requiring separate instruments for later works require those instruments; an automatic record is not a universal substitute.

After that initial acceptance, covered intentional submissions are assigned by default unless the Contributor clearly excludes an identified submission **before or at the time of submitting it**, using the agreed `Rights-Assignment: excluded` marker or an equivalent express notice identifying the full commit SHA(s). Excluded submissions are not Contributions under this assignment and must not be merged as Company-owned code without separate rights clearance. An exclusion is not a commercial license to Company code and does not reverse a transfer already effective for a previous submission.

## 2. Present assignment of economic copyright

In exchange for the Company's binding grant in section 4 and subject to any mandatory compensation terms recorded in Schedule A, Contributor **hereby assigns** to NUTRIFIT LLC all transferable economic copyright rights that Contributor owns in an identified Contribution, worldwide, for the full duration of those rights, subject to nonwaivable statutory rights. For identified Contributions already submitted in the accepting PR, assignment takes effect upon express acceptance and identification through the process. For a subsequent Contribution covered by the expressly accepted electronic submission process, the transfer is intended to take effect upon its intentional identifiable submission through that process, to the extent legally permitted. Mandatory additional formalities must be satisfied before merge.

The assignment includes rights to reproduce, modify, prepare derivative works, translate, distribute, display, communicate and make available the Contribution; to license, sublicense or transfer those rights; and to use it in commercial or noncommercial products and services under terms selected by the Company. It does not assign rights in excluded third-party or preexisting material, nor rights Contributor does not own.

Where an economic right cannot presently be assigned but may lawfully be licensed, Contributor grants the Company an exclusive, transferable, sublicensable license to exercise that identified right for the same purposes and term, to the maximum lawful extent. Any necessary jurisdiction-specific instrument must be signed before the affected Contribution is merged; this fallback is not a claim to remove mandatory restrictions.

## 3. Authorship and statutory rights

This agreement does not falsely designate the Company as the human author. Authorship remains attributable to the actual author. Contributor consents, to the extent law permits, to ordinary software changes, combinations, translations and publication of the Contribution. Nontransferable or nonwaivable moral rights are preserved.

Nothing waives a statutory termination right that cannot be waived, including an applicable right under 17 U.S.C. § 203, or any mandatory remuneration right. Neither party claims that a voluntary contribution automatically qualifies as a work made for hire. Existing grants that survive under applicable law must be disclosed in Schedule A.

## 4. Company grant back; no commercial permission to Contributor

On effectiveness, the Company grants Contributor a worldwide, nonexclusive, royalty-free license to the assigned Contribution alone to copy, modify, execute and distribute it for the permitted noncommercial research and product purposes described in the NutriFit Noncommercial Research License 1.0, whose exact text is attached to the electronic acceptance record referenced in Schedule A, subject to its restrictions and notices. This contractual grant back is consideration for the assignment and does not depend on whether the Company merges or publishes the Contribution.

It does not grant rights to unrelated Company code. Contributor receives no commercial license, ownership share, royalties or promise of employment, acceptance, publication, attribution beyond legally required authorship, or future compensation except what Schedule A expressly states or mandatory law requires.

**Contributor expressly understands that the Company may commercially use and relicense the Contribution, while Contributor and other public users receive only the granted noncommercial permissions.** The Company may retain revenue without sharing it unless a separate signed agreement or mandatory law requires otherwise.

### 4.1. Discretionary recognition and rewards

After evaluating a Contribution, the Company may, at its discretion, award a voluntary one-time monetary payment or another form of recognition. The Company determines whether to award anything and, if so, its amount, form, eligibility and proposed payment terms. No reward is guaranteed by submission, assignment, review, merge, adoption, publication, use or commercialization of a Contribution.

This possibility does not create an entitlement to payment, royalties, revenue share, equity, employment or a minimum reward. Any specifically approved award and its binding conditions must be communicated separately in writing. Payment details are requested privately only after an award is approved; the contributor need not provide bank details to submit code.

A voluntary reward is separate from the section 4 grant-back consideration. Absence of a voluntary reward does not by itself reverse an effective assignment. Nothing in this section removes mandatory compensation or other rights that applicable law does not allow parties to waive.

## 6. Ownership and authority assurances

Contributor represents that they have capacity and authority to execute this agreement, own the assigned rights or are duly authorized by their owner, have identified third-party and preexisting materials and prior grants, and are not knowingly violating another person's rights or confidentiality obligations. ## Schedule A — electronic contribution record

For an ordinary individual contribution, Schedule A consists of the linked electronic acceptance and contribution coverage receipts created through the repository process. They identify:

1. Repository, agreement version and exact text, grant-back license exact text, both text hashes, combined acceptance hash and immutable document links.
2. Contributor's authenticated public GitHub account ID and login, acceptance comment, intentional signature statement and acceptance time. Acceptance incorporates the ownership and authority assurances in section 6.
3. PR URL, head SHA, full covered commit SHA(s), authored contribution scope as identified by the files and patches in those immutable commits, and coverage record time.
4. Any declared third-party, preexisting, excluded or previously licensed material and applicable terms, as identified in the PR and separately retained records. No notice may purport to assign rights Contributor does not own.
5. Consideration: section 4 grant back; no additional payment is promised by default. Approved voluntary awards or mandatory remuneration terms are recorded separately when applicable.

Actual organizational ownership, representative authority, jurisdiction-specific formalities, known patent limitations or mandatory compensation that cannot be established by the ordinary individual process require additional identified records before merge. The Company must not treat the automatic status as proof of rights it does not hold. Public account identity alone is not proof of ownership or authority. Confidential identity, authority and payment evidence is retained privately.
